# Numberlink

Connect matching numbers. Paths can't cross. Fill every cell.

**[Play it in your browser →](https://jonahfrancisbenedicto.github.io/numberlink/)**

</div>

<img alt="Numberlink: an 8×8 puzzle in progress" src="docs/media/hero-light.png" />

<div align="center">
  <img src="docs/media/play.gif" width="420" alt="Solving a 6×6 puzzle by dragging paths between matching numbers" />
</div>

- **16 board types**, each with its own twist, from plain grids to boards with bridges, portals, loops, stones and colours
- **960 levels**, 60 for each board type, and every puzzle has exactly one solution
- **Infinite**: endless puzzles of every type and size, in Normal, Hard and Expert. Harder ones open as you play
- **Daily**: a new puzzle every day, timed, with a streak that forgives one missed day a week and a result to share
- **Weekly**: one endless run a week, the same for everyone. Every puzzle is harder than the last, with three minutes each. Afterwards, look back through the run and play its puzzles again
- **Tutorial**: a lesson for each board type that shows every move on the board
- **Progress**: your records for each way to play, with half a year of dailies as a calendar
- **Customise**: a Classic or Mono theme, in light or dark, to try on a board before you choose
- Your best moves and time are kept for every level, and hints are earned by solving without them
- Calm sounds and lo-fi music, and keyboard shortcuts with a command palette
