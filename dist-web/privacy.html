Privacy

Numberlink · October 6, 2026

Numberlink collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What's stored

Your progress (solved levels, your daily streak, unfinished boards and settings) is saved on your device only. It never leaves it. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it.

What's sent

Nothing. Every puzzle is made on your device, and the app makes no network requests while you play. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children either.

Questions

Ask on the project's GitHub issues. If this policy ever changes, this page will say so, with a new date.
